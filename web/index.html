How it works

It looks at where you are going,
not just at what is nearby.

01

Perceive

Every road user is detected, tracked and placed on a top-down map of the road ahead.

02

Anticipate

Motion is followed over time to tell a pedestrian walking beside the road from one stepping into it.

03

Decide

Seventeen candidate paths are scored every frame. The answer stays simple: go, slow down or brake — with the reason.

Oncoming traffic on its own side keeps moving.
A pedestrian on the footpath keeps walking.
Only a conflict with your path changes the decision.
